1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how OSRS Tracker collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and any related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We do not ask for or store any account or profile information. The Service accesses Old School RuneScape hiscores through an API provided by Jagex Limited, which do not contain any personal information.

Our servers log every request they receive, including your IP address, the page or resource requested, the referring page, your browser's user agent and the time of the request. We use these logs only to keep the Service secure and working, for example to detect and block automated attacks and abuse.

The Service may store certain data in the local storage of your device, such as your favorite players. This data is collected and stored to improve your user experience and enable certain features within the Service.

3. Cookies

OSRS Tracker uses Google Analytics 4, which utilizes first-party cookies to distinguish unique users and sessions. These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website and improve user experience. No personal information is collected through these cookies.

By using the Service, you consent to the use of these first-party cookies for the purposes stated.

4. Use of Information

We use the information collected to understand how the Service is being used and to improve it further. Server logs, including IP addresses, are used only for security and to diagnose problems. Processing them is based on our legitimate interest in protecting the Service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Sharing of Information

We do not share any data with third parties, except as required by Google Analytics to provide us with insights on site usage, visitor count, visited pages, and other relevant data.

We use CrowdSec to block malicious traffic. When an IP address is detected attacking the Service (for example by scanning for vulnerabilities), that IP address, the type of attack and the time are shared with CrowdSec's community blocklist, which protects other sites too. Normal visits are never shared.

6. Data Retention

We retain server logs, including IP addresses, for a maximum of 30 days, after which they are deleted automatically. Since we do not have accounts or sessions, we do not link requests to a person.

We encourage you to review the Privacy Policy whenever you use the Service to stay informed about our information practices and the ways you can help protect your privacy. Your continued use of the Service after any changes to the Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users from the European Union have certain rights regarding their personal data. The only personal data we hold are the IP addresses in our server logs, which are kept solely for security and deleted automatically after 30 days.

You can ask which log entries we hold for your IP address. Because these logs are needed to detect and investigate abuse, we generally do not delete or change individual log entries on request, or stop logging a specific IP address, before they expire (Articles 17(1)(c) and 21(1) GDPR).
